Maxwell residual time differences divide directly by the timestep, including at subnormal timesteps. Both spatial and residual time differences divide terms separately if finite samples have an overflowing difference, and reject an unrepresentable derivative. These measures do not guarantee accuracy for arbitrary extreme-scale histories.

FourVelocity.fromVelocity3(v,c) rejects |v|≥c, constructs a normalized future-directed four-velocity, and exposes gamma, toVelocity3, and validate. relativeVelocity(other) returns the three-velocity of other after transforming it into this observer's instantaneous rest frame; compatible batch shapes broadcast. Construction preserves Lib4's distinction between shape validity and physical validation, so use validate() when accepting externally supplied four-velocities. Mechanics tensors are bounded to one million components and rank eight. FourMomentum.fromMassVelocity(m,v,c) stores (E/c,p) and broadcasts compatible mass/velocity batch dimensions. energy, momentum3, and invariantMass preserve batches; boost accepts a single or broadcast batch of Lorentz matrices and validates each applied matrix. Invariant-mass roundoff tolerance scales with momentum magnitude. With clampRoundoff:false, JavaScript still throws for a small negative residual within tolerance to avoid returning NaN; Lib4's direct square root can return NaN for that opt-out case. FourCurrent.fromChargeCurrent(rho,j,{c}) stores (cρ,j) and broadcasts compatible leading dimensions of rho and j; chargeDensity and spatialCurrent preserve those dimensions. transform broadcasts a single or batched Lorentz matrix over the current batch and validates every applied matrix. The local Lib4 FourCurrent API has no separate field-coupling method. MaxwellEquations accepts a FourCurrent value as its grid source when its batch and spatial shapes match the field grid.

Lib4 frozen dataclass value objects freeze their JavaScript instance fields after construction: Event4, SpacetimeInterval, LightCone, SpatialSlice, InertialFrame, Lorentz/Poincare transforms, mechanics values, and MaxwellEquations. This matches dataclass field reassignment behavior; nested component arrays remain ordinary mutable JavaScript arrays, as Lib4's contained tensor data is mutable too.

RelativityContext keeps c, units, epsilon0, and a validated metric together. Natural units require c=1 and default to epsilon0=1/(4π). Factories for vectors, covectors, tensors, events, intervals, frames, light cones, spatial slices, four-velocity, four-momentum, four-current, electromagnetic fields, Maxwell equations, and retarded fields are available. Factories that depend on c or epsilon0 propagate those context constants; representative algebra and mechanics factories are tested directly. setUnits returns a context with new constants but does not convert existing values. toDevice returns a context carrying the requested device label; plain JavaScript arrays do not move to a device, and WebGPU work remains explicit through WebGPUBackend. Lib4's PyTorch dtype/device placement and tensor-transfer semantics are not implemented.

EMFieldTensor.fromEB(E,B,{c,epsilon0}) combines the electric and magnetic fields into an antisymmetric tensor and broadcasts compatible field batch dimensions. Field accessors, invariants, dual, Lorentz transformation, Lorentz force, and stress-energy preserve batches; tensor and transform dimensions broadcast where applicable. invariants() returns named magneticMinusElectricOverC2 and electricDotMagnetic values, each with the field batch shape; Lib4 returns the same two values as a tuple. Batched JavaScript results previously used an array of objects. The pseudoscalar uses scaled arithmetic when intermediate electric components or products overflow or underflow; extreme cancellation and scale disparities can still lose precision. Applied Lorentz matrices are validated, and inputs are bounded to one million scalar components and rank eight.

MaxwellEquations.evolve(E,B,{spacing,dt,steps,current}) uses periodic forward/backward Yee-style differences with electric samples at whole times and magnetic samples at half times. It returns [electricTimes,electricHistory,magneticTimes,magneticHistory]. Up to seven leading batch axes are preserved after the history time axis; electric, magnetic, and supplied current grids must have matching batch and spatial shapes. A current may be supplied as a spatial-current grid [...,Nx,Ny,Nz,3] or four-current grid [...,Nx,Ny,Nz,4] in (cρ,j) order, or as a compatible batched FourCurrent. maxwellResiduals returns discrete Ampère and Faraday history residuals; constraintResiduals returns Gauss electric and magnetic residuals with discrete charge continuity. Each grid is limited to 32,768 cells and total stored history to 1,000,000 scalar field components across all batches and times. Residual and constraint APIs preflight the combined history shape against this limit before traversing and copying frame data. Nested grid/history arrays must be acyclic and use no more than twelve dimensions. The 3D Yee CFL condition is checked. When c squared overflows, underflows, or becomes subnormal, electric updates and Ampère residuals use scaled products. Current coupling avoids explicitly forming an unrepresentable inverse permittivity; the electric Gauss residual also rescales charge continuity when permittivity is subnormal. This does not guarantee accuracy for all extreme grid values. The implementation does not reproduce PyTorch device/autograd behavior or component-specific Yee offsets. RetardedField.retardedTime(event) and retardedPotential(event) accept event arrays shaped (...,4) and preserve their leading dimensions; evaluate and sample use the same batched potential contract. Root finding checks the lower endpoint of a distance-based bracket before bounded bisection and rejects a clear bracket failure, which may indicate an invalid speed bound. This endpoint check does not prove the bound along the whole trajectory. Aggregate batch root-work is capped at one million trajectory evaluations. If floating-point time spacing prevents further bracket refinement before the requested tolerance is reached, it returns an exactly represented root when found and otherwise rejects. Source speed is checked against both c and its declared bound. Point-charge potential is singular on the source worldline; separations indistinguishable from zero at the coordinates' floating-point scale also reject. Small nonzero separations near the origin are not rejected solely because they are small in absolute units. Non-finite observation times, root brackets or residuals, and potential components are rejected; representable results at extreme scales are not generally guaranteed. PyTorch device/dtype/autograd behavior is not represented.

For physically meaningful stress-energy calculations, supply a symmetric, nonsingular covariant metric. Raw matrices are accepted for compatibility with Lib4; lowering both field indices uses the full G F Gᵀ contraction even if a raw matrix is not symmetric. Nonsymmetric matrices are not physical metrics in this model.

Shared context

new RelativityContext({metric,c,units,epsilon0,device}) groups constants and factories. Use createVector, createCovector, createTensor, createEvent, and createSpacetimeInterval with their component inputs. Use createInertialFrame(origin,{orientation,relativeVelocity}), createLightCone(apex,{branch,atol}), and createSpatialSlice(origin,normal,basis,options) for spacetime objects. Mechanics factories include createFourVelocity(components), fourVelocityFromVelocity3(v), createFourMomentum(components), fourMomentumFromMassVelocity(m,v), createFourCurrent(rho,j), createEMFieldTensor(E,B), createMaxwellEquations(), and createRetardedField(position,velocity,charge,maxSpeed,options). Context-dependent constants are supplied by the context. context.setUnits(units,options) and context.toDevice(label) each return a new context without converting existing arrays or values.

Electric-field extraction and Lorentz force reject non-finite outputs. Lorentz force uses scaled four-term products to retain representable results when naive intermediate multiplication overflows. Batched regression checks that four-force is orthogonal to four-velocity. Metric4 inputs to stress-energy calculations must retain their constructed batch shape.

The electromagnetic dual accepts tensors admitted by the electromagnetic constructor’s relative antisymmetry tolerance; the antisymmetric contraction determines the result.

Four-current charge-density and spatial-current extraction validate the current component arrays; charge density rejects an unrepresentable non-finite result.

Four-momentum and four-velocity accessors validate mutable component arrays; energy and gamma reject non-finite outputs.

Invariant mass normalizes momentum before division when individual component-to-c ratios overflow, preserving selected representable near-null results.

FourVelocity.validate({atol,rtol}) uses an absolute plus relative mass-shell tolerance. At very small c, a large absolute tolerance relative to c² can admit a null or otherwise off-shell vector; choose tolerances appropriate to the units and scale. If c² underflows to zero and normalizing components overflows, representable absolute comparisons are retained when the absolute tolerance is at least the smallest normal number.

When a Maxwell electric update overflows an intermediate term, evolution retries with scaled current and wave terms, and rejects a non-finite resulting electric field. This recovers selected finite updates but does not guarantee preservation of every extreme cancellation.

Maxwell evolution and residual APIs reject non-finite output grids, including magnetic histories and scalar constraint residuals. Intermediate overflow can still prevent a representable final result.

Maxwell evolution rejects time configurations whose final electric timestamp overflows or whose first magnetic half-step rounds to zero; the residual APIs accept their independently supplied histories and timestep.

MaxwellEquations.gridCoordinates({shape,origin,spacing}) returns conventional periodic Yee sample positions for interpreting the solver’s forward and backward differences. electric[0..2] and magnetic[0..2] each contain coordinate grids shaped [Nx,Ny,Nz,3]; the index selects the field component. Electric components occupy edges offset by one half-cell along their own axes, while magnetic components occupy faces offset by one half-cell along the other two axes. charge gives node positions for backward electric divergence and magneticDivergence gives cell-center positions for forward magnetic divergence. Origin defaults to zero and spacing to one; spatial dimensions must each be at least two and the grid remains limited to 32,768 cells. Positions can lie half a cell beyond the last node, representing locations on the periodic grid. These coordinates describe the discrete solver placement, not an interpolation of its fields.

MaxwellEquations.collocateFields(E,Bbefore,Bafter) averages one electric frame and the bracketing magnetic half-time frames to charge nodes at the electric time. It uses periodic spatial neighbors: two electric edge samples per component and four magnetic face samples per component, followed by the average of the two magnetic times. All three inputs must have the same (...,Nx,Ny,Nz,3) shape, with each spatial dimension at least two; matching leading batch dimensions are preserved. The per-grid cell limit and a one-million-scalar aggregate output limit apply. For evolve histories, use E[n], B[n-1], and B[n] for an interior electric frame n. This is linear interpolation and smooth-field accuracy is not guaranteed near discontinuities; floating-point underflow may still affect extreme values. The returned {electric,magnetic} arrays can be passed to EMFieldTensor.fromEB for node-aligned derived quantities.

A periodic vacuum plane-wave regression combines evolve and collocateFields at an interior time and observes near second-order magnetic-field convergence for 8, 16, and 32 samples per wavelength. This verifies that particular smooth case, not accuracy for every field or grid.

MaxwellEquations.collocateHistory(Ehistory,BhalfHistory) performs this interpolation for every interior electric frame, returning {frameIndices,electric,magnetic}. The indices identify the corresponding entries of the original electric history; use them to select the original electric timestamps. Histories must have matching (frames,...,Nx,Ny,Nz,3) shapes, with at least three electric frames and exactly one fewer magnetic half-frame. Output retains leading batch dimensions and is limited to one million scalar components. End electric frames have no two bracketing magnetic half-frames and are omitted.

RetardedField.retardedTime treats only zero representable source-observer separation as coincident; a small but representable offset remains a distinct event even at large spatial coordinates. The potential calculation retains its conservative near-source rejection at the current coordinate scale, and bisection accuracy remains limited by the supplied tolerance and representable time spacing.
