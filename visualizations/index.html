    Reading the model

    The spacetime coordinate convention is x⁰ = ct with metric signature (−,+,+,+). Axes are labeled in normalized units unless stated otherwise.

    • Exact: direct algebraic relationships evaluated from the stated model.
    • Numerical: sampled or integrated approximations with finite resolution.
    • Pedagogical: a simplified geometry chosen to show a concept, not a calibrated astrophysical solution.
