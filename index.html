Einstein Relativity

Offline-first library and visual demonstrations for special and general relativity.

Explore the visualizations

  1. Minkowski spacetime — Compare stationary and moving observers' worldlines, then switch frames to see Lorentz-transformed coordinates.
  2. Light cone as an evolving sphere — Watch spherical light-front slices contract to the apex and expand into the future.
  3. Lorentz contraction and simultaneity — See rod-end worldlines and compare the simultaneity slices used to measure length.
  4. Twin-paradox worldlines — Follow a stay-at-home clock and a traveling clock through their reunion and compare elapsed proper time.
  5. Velocity addition and aberration — See how a boost changes light directions across a star field, with playback highlighting each sampled direction.
  6. Electric and magnetic field mixing — Compare boosted electric and magnetic vectors and their invariants; playback scans between their fixed tips.
  7. Charged-particle motion — Follow a numerically integrated test charge moving through a uniform magnetic field.
  8. Gravitational lensing rays — Watch light paths bend around a central mass in a simplified weak-field model.
  9. Curved-space embedding grid — Read surface curvature from color and radial metric stretch from wire-grid spacing as a playback marker scans the radius.
  10. Animated 4D field explorer — Explore a moving plane-wave slice with field vectors, observer paths, and isosurfaces.
